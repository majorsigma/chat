# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 2025_

Thank you for using GChat! Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

## Information We Collect

We only collect the following information when you use GChat:
- **Name:** Used to identify you to other users on the platform.
- **Email:** Used for authentication, account management, and communication if required.
- **Password:** Used exclusively for secure authentication.

## How We Use Your Information

- **Authentication:** Your email and password are used to create and secure your GChat account.
- **Identification:** Your name is displayed to other users to identify you during chat sessions.
- **Security:** Passwords are stored securely using industry-standard encryption/hashing techniques.

## Data Sharing

- We **do not** share your personal information with third parties.
- Your data is used solely for account management and chat functionality within GChat.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

## User Control

- You can update your name or email within the app (feature permitting).
- You may delete your account; this will remove your profile and associated data from our systems.

## Account Deletion

You may request to delete your GChat account and all associated data at any time.  
To do so, [click here to request account deletion](mailto:support@gchatapp.com?subject=Account%20Deletion%20Request&body=Please%20delete%20my%20account%20and%20associated%20data.).

Alternatively, you can send an email to: **olalekan.o.ogundele.com** with the subject "Account Deletion Request."  
We will process your request within 7 days and remove your information from our systems.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted here with an updated revision date.

## Contact Us

If you have concerns or questions about your privacy, please contact the app maintainer.
